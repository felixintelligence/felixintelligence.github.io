FelixSourcingBot

The web crawler for Felix, a research tool that maps independent physician practices.

If you found this page in your server logs, our bot visited your site. Its User-Agent is:

FelixSourcingBot/1.0 (+https://felixintelligence.github.io/bot/)

What it does

It visits the public website of a medical practice to confirm business facts the practice publishes about itself: its name, address, phone number, specialty and locations. It looks for the practice's own site, not for patients and not for individuals.

How it behaves

  • At most one request per second to any site.
  • It reads robots.txt first and skips the site if a rule disallows it, or if a crawl delay is longer than its own interval.
  • Public pages only. It never logs in, submits forms, accepts terms or keeps cookies.
  • It reads text only and stores the facts it extracts. It does not keep copies of your pages, text or images.

What it never collects

  • Patient information of any kind.
  • Personal contact details of physicians or staff, such as a personal phone number, personal email or home address.
  • Anything behind a login or a patient portal.

How to block it

Add this to your site's robots.txt:

User-agent: FelixSourcingBot
Disallow: /

The bot obeys this rule on its next visit. You can also email us and we will exclude your site.
